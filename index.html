Free IT certification study tools

A plain index of the free tools, cheat sheets and reference data on DailyDebian. Everything runs in your browser and needs no sign-up. Useful for CompTIA Network+, Security+ and A+, Cisco CCNA, Linux, cloud and PMP study.

Calculators and practice

  • Subnet mask calculatorNetwork, broadcast, host range, mask, wildcard and binary for any IPv4 address and prefix, plus subnet splitting.
  • Subnetting practiceUnlimited random questions at three levels, with worked answers.
  • CIDR calculator and VLSM plannerHost ranges, VLSM layouts, IP range to CIDR and route summarisation.
  • IPv6 subnet calculatorExpand and compress addresses, find a prefix's first and last address, split into /64 or other subnets.
  • Wildcard mask calculatorSubnet mask and CIDR to wildcard mask and back, and what an ACL entry matches.
  • Binary, decimal and hex converterConvert between number bases and turn an IPv4 address into binary.
  • chmod calculatorLinux permissions as numbers, letters and the exact chmod command, including SUID, SGID and sticky bit.
  • Umask calculatorDefault file and directory permissions for any umask, or the umask for the permissions you want.
  • Cron expression builderBuild a 5-field crontab schedule, read it in plain English, see the next 5 run times.
  • Unix timestamp converterEpoch seconds or milliseconds to UTC and local time, and back.
  • CVSS calculator (v3.1 and v4.0)Scores and severity from the metrics or a vector string, using FIRST's published formulas.
  • Risk calculator (SLE, ALE, ARO)Single and annualized loss expectancy, and whether a control pays for itself.
  • Uptime and SLA calculatorAllowed downtime per day, week, month and year for any availability percentage.
  • RAID calculatorUsable capacity and drive failures survived for RAID 0, 1, 5, 6 and 10.
  • Earned value and PERT calculatorSV, CV, SPI, CPI, EAC, ETC, VAC and TCPI, plus PERT three-point estimates, for PMP and CAPM.
  • Ports quiz10 random port-number questions with an explanation after each answer.
  • OSI model quiz10 questions on the 7 layers, PDUs, devices and protocols.
